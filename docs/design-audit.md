# Design consistency audit — 5 October 2026

The previous review was incomplete. Its servo reference repeated the housing's wrong ear datum, its motion checks covered less axial movement than its tolerance notes, and its common-offset checks did not establish relative square alignment. The user supplied an accurate diagram and a physical failure; the model and validation were responsible.

This audit uses **Limo CAD's native recomputation, retained-solid interference checks, associative drawing exports and live viewport captures**. [Raw direct tool results](../cad-design-audit.json) retain failed controls and explicitly identify the source of each model. [Current nominal tool results](../cad-tool-validation.json) and [repeatable component checks](../preprint-review.json) accompany the files. Separately derived inspection budgets are labeled separately from native measurements.

## Findings and corrections

**Servo datum: confirmed cause of the failed printed housing.** The diagram's 24.7 mm starts at the mounting ear's underside. Both the former reference and housing used its opposite face, producing a false clearance pass and placing a real seated servo 1.2 mm beyond the rear cavity. The corrected ear faces are Y162.8/165.5 and their pockets Y162.6/165.7. Native dimensions measure 2.70 mm ears, 24.70 mm underside-to-rear, 40.50 ×20.00 ×37.50 mm conservative case envelope, 41.30 ×20.80 mm clamshell pocket and 1.50 mm nominal rear room. [Independent drawing-envelope regression](../servo-fit-verification.json) retains the old failure. The shown dimensions are not requested again.

**Flight movement: validation coverage was inconsistent.** The previous sampled check and derived entrance bound used ±0.30 mm, while the conservative collar stack allowed ±0.50 mm. Merely moving the auger 0.50 mm into the nominal stop would be an invalid tolerance test. The explicit inspection envelope shifts the collar faces −0.10 mm and rear housing stop +0.10 mm so the auger reaches a possible displaced stop. The entrance radii are then reduced for the documented 0.02 mm verification tessellation, 0.08 mm manufacturing export, two 0.10 mm radial surface allowances, and two further 0.10 mm axial face allowances acting through the taper slope.

At the former Ø33.2 entrance, the native tool measured **0.148745 mm** minimum flight clearance in that sampled envelope, below the existing 0.20 mm review target. The entrance is now **Ø33.6 at Y107.8**, tapering to the unchanged **Ø31.2 at Y113.7** in the same revolve. The collar opening, thrust stops, sleeve stops and feature count remain unchanged. Current-source native envelope samples measure **0.250783 mm minimum**, including four diagonal offsets of 0.40 mm in X and Z (0.565685 mm radial), with the last flight rotated toward each corner. The separately derived tessellation screening bound is approximately 0.250783 mm normal to the taper. It is not a native tolerance certificate.

**Square alignment: housing clearance was mistaken for mating clearance.** Moving only the servo and adapter 0.30 mm sideways while keeping the auger centered produces **13.210147 mm³ native square-mate interference**. Moving the auger to follow the adapter clears it. Six sampled coupled assemblies include the actual servo, both housing halves, both auger bodies, adapter and floating sleeve; they have no unintended volumetric interference. Engage the square first and let the auger and loose sleeve follow the servo axis before closing the clamshell. Check free axial sliding and rotation. The 0.50 mm common housing-axis allowance is not permission for 0.50 mm relative peg/socket misalignment. Relative tilt, actual surface errors and loaded deflection have not been automatically validated.

**Installed axial datum: one transfer allowance was omitted.** The former ±0.40 mm absolute-coordinate estimate was also presented as a measurement from the printed front thrust face. That face has its own 0.10 mm allowance. The conservative installed budget is now **27.40 ±0.50 mm from the actual front chassis thrust face**, including ear gap, ear-stop extrema, adapter seat-to-flange extrema and datum transfer. Nominal world Y coordinates locate the CAD model; they are not physical inspection datums. Downstream conditional bounds are updated together: **8.80–11.20 mm square engagement, 0.80 mm minimum peg-tip room, 0.50 mm minimum hub/flange gap and 0.50 mm minimum adapter/housing rear gap**. The Ø29.6 rear chamber ending at Y153.6 accommodates this allowance without another geometry change.

**Feedback and materials: records now reflect reality.** The previous housing failed a physical test; the corrected housing has not yet passed one. Both facts are recorded. CAD/portable materials remain PETG for the housing, auger and adapter, and PLA for the loose sleeve. CAD colors are yellow #E5AE3C, orange #D57E37, gray #BEC3CA and teal #37AAA1. Bambu's actual saved spool palette remains #034638, #FF671F, #FFFFFF and #DCF478 with its PETG Basic /PLA Glow profiles. These palettes have different purposes and are both explicit. No reported loaded strength is inferred from infill or a clearance pass.

## Hardware and tool limits

Four ruthex RX-M3×5.7 inserts retain Ø4.00 ×6.70 mm pilots, with at least 3.07 mm checked surrounding wall after the stated allowance. Two front M3×12 and two rear M3×18 screws have 4.0/5.3 mm nominal insert engagement. The temporary native center-screw envelope clears all printed parts; the retained adapter web is 2.60 mm.

The servo drawing does not show cable-boot dimensions, usable internal thread depth or factory screw length. The purchased reference has no modeled rotating spline shaft or internal threaded cavity. **The tool cannot check those absent surfaces.** M3×6 remains an inspected trial, with a required usable hole depth of at least 4.00 mm. The Ø5.70 pilot remains provisional pending the printed coupon fit. Its 3.70 mm depth is a Hitec H25T family reference, not an INJORA-published universal standard. No new supplier dimensions are invented.

The available interface has **no automatic GD&T stack validator**; the saved production assembly has no joint-driven motion study. Native sampled interference is exact for the retained solids at the stated poses; it does not prove every combination of manufacturing errors, relative tilt or load. Critical ±0.10 mm dimensions, surface controls, collar/sleeve lengths and capture spans are acceptance requirements, not measured printer capability. Hole-summary inference also misclassifies some open corner arcs and outer cylinders, leaves depths unknown and emits warnings. Those warnings and print-layout warnings remain in the raw nominal report. Preflight `ok: true` is not treated as a universal manufacturing pass.

## Printable exterior after support review

The 2.80 mm suspended servo floor was rejected because it generated support underneath the body. The current exterior has a flat bed face, a 10.20 mm servo floor, smooth tangent ear blends and bed-supported handles with 45-degree shoulders. The handle rear edge aligns with the body, removing its small rear step. Source and inspection copies recompute identically; all five other native bodies and seven non-housing STLs remain unchanged. Native dimensions measure selected current planes; no global minimum-wall or GD&T validator is exposed.

Native Bambu slices both housing halves with zero support toolpaths and only PETG, at 6 h 54 min /220.0 g. The lower half uses 3 h 26 min /109.4 g and independently produces zero supports with automatic supports enabled. The upper screw-recess roofs retain short bridges. Support generation is disabled only for housing objects; this setting is explicit and does not certify bridge quality. Verify these roofs physically. The global user printer/material/process/purge settings remain unchanged. The support-used metadata can describe configuration instead of actual extrusion, so native feature times, G-code roles and material use are checked.

It also exposed a rear-pad join partially refilling the boot bay. The existing cut is reordered after all housing joins; one existing sketch supplies the complete curved route. The current user-selected rear outlet replaces the side swoop with one tangent R8 trial turn, passing the rear screw and crossing the rear face at X28/Y206.5. The side-routing bulge is removed. Feature count remains 71, and other printable parts are unchanged.

Native continuous Ø4/Ø5 sweeps fit; an oversized Ø6.2 sweep fails. The nominal Ø4 envelope has 1.00 mm minimum housing clearance. Sampled cable placement, insertion and cover closure also pass. The rear insert closest to the full boot bay now retains **3.07 mm sampled wall after allowance**, replacing the former 4.45 mm claim. This is section sampling, not a native global wall certificate. Current Bambu estimates and support evidence above include this change.
