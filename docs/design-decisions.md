# Current design decisions

Use these decisions when rebuilding or reviewing the current release. Historical alternatives in Git and supplier reference files do not select a different design.

- The latest [user-supplied INJORA diagram](../source/references/user-servo-fit-diagram.png) controls the 41.5 mm tip-to-rear, 37.5 mm case height, **24.7 mm underside-of-ear-to-rear** and 2.7 mm ear thickness. Both the servo reference and housing had placed the wrong ear face at 24.7 mm; the corrected ear pocket is Y162.6–165.7. The case pocket clears the larger catalog plan length 40.5 mm with 0.4 mm on each side, giving 41.3 ×20.8 mm. Do not request the shown dimensions again. [Source provenance](../source/references/provenance.json) records the drawing and its separate catalog summary.
- One PETG adapter seats directly on the servo's metal shaft, retained by its compatible center screw. Its round pilot has no printed spline teeth. There is no separate metal horn or M4 mounting pattern.
- The pilot is provisionally Ø5.70 ×3.70 mm. Select the diameter from the four physical fit coupons. The [Hitec H25T depth reference](../source/references/hitec-horn-spline-2026.pdf), printed page 75, supplies the 3.70 mm design depth; it is not a universal servo standard.
- Keep the 14 mm rounded square peg, 14.4 mm socket, Ø24 adapter boss, Ø28 ×5 mm flange and full loose PLA sleeve. The clamshell alone captures the collar and sleeve. No ring lock or clip is needed.
- Use the current housing with its shared entrance taper and correctly located 3.10 mm ear pocket. The rear adapter chamber ends at Y153.6, accommodating the ear-clearance and printed-position stack. Preserve the locating features, screw seats and four Ø4.00 ×6.70 mm insert pilots for ruthex RX-M3×5.7 inserts.
- Use a continuous flat exterior bed face under both halves. The servo floor is 10.20 mm, bearing floor 2.80 mm nominal, and selected ear-side walls 3.80 mm. Tangent native arcs blend around the ears; do not restore stepped ear bulges or the suspended 2.80 mm floor relief. Handles have 11.50 mm bed lands and 45-degree shoulders, with their rear edge aligned to the body. Their assembled depth is 41.20 mm. The model has 71 active entries. Nominal dimensions are not global wall or strength certification.
- Preserve saved Bambu global printer, material, strength, support and purge settings. Set support generation off only for the two housing objects; the upper screw-recess roofs must bridge and need physical print verification. A native automatic-support probe confirms the lower geometry needs no supports. The housing plate requires purge-tower X180/Y142; this is a layout correction, not a process-profile change. The lower-half test file keeps its independently checked single-half tower location.
- The current entrance is Ø33.6 to Ø31.2 in the existing revolve. Check ±0.50 mm tolerance-extreme auger travel separately from ±0.30 mm nominal travel. Native envelope samples also cover the nominal servo-pocket diagonal offset of 0.566 mm. [Consistency audit](design-audit.md) records the prior validation failure and the limits of this coverage.
- Installed flange allowance is ±0.50 mm from the actual front chassis thrust face: 0.20 ear gap +0.10 ear stop +0.10 seat-to-flange +0.10 datum transfer. The conservative square stack gives 8.80–11.20 mm engagement, at least 0.80 mm tip gap and 0.50 mm hub-to-flange gap. These are conditional inspection limits, not native GD&T certification or measured print capability.
- Engage the square before nesting the assembly. The auger and loose sleeve must follow the servo drive axis. Common housing-axis clearance does not certify relative square misalignment; a native 0.30 mm independent-offset control collided. Inspect free axial sliding, alignment and rotation.
- CAD design colors and saved slicer spool colors have distinct purposes; both remain explicit in [README](../README.md). The Bambu project contains current object overrides. Portable 3MF exports carry geometry and material metadata; STLs carry only geometry.

The previous housing failed the user's physical fit test. Physical fit of the corrected housing remains untested. Remaining checks are printed fit and seating, usable internal screw depth and factory screw compatibility, cable boot fit, free rotation, alignment, loaded torque, creep and wear. CAD checks do not establish a measured strength rating. These checks do not reopen the confirmed nominal hardware dimensions.

The separate CAD application team owns the 3MF feature work tracked in [Limo-CAD issues #312–#316](https://github.com/jackControls/Limo-CAD/issues/312). This repository continues to use its existing export and Bambu preparation scripts until those capabilities are implemented and verified.

Our own packaging script's reset of user object settings is [project issue #1](https://github.com/jeffglousher/garden-auger-technic/issues/1); the related user feedback is attached to [3MF handoff #316](https://github.com/jackControls/Limo-CAD/issues/316#issuecomment-6035859200). The [issue index](../experiments/upstream-issue-index.json) records evidence, categories and links. Keep experiments and findings committed and pushed, and file issues rather than experiment PRs. Implementation waits until the owner confirms [Bevy finalization](https://github.com/jackControls/Limo-CAD/issues/38). Observed application bugs need reproduction against that finalized build before a fix is claimed; current experiments remain external project tooling.

The cable exits the rear face toward the controls through a 6 ×8 mm split-seam route with one tangent R8 trial bend. The side outlet and its exterior bulge are superseded by the user's rear-routing preference. The rear outlet is centered at X28/Y206.5 and clears the screw at X16/Y196.8. The old straight route ended inside a handle, and a later pad join partly filled its boot bay. Reuse and reorder the existing cable cut after all housing joins. Keep the connector outside. Bundle, boot, seam entry and minimum bend radius remain trial assumptions requiring physical fit. The [native cable experiment](../cable-route-verification.json) tests continuous Ø4/Ø5 swept envelopes and rejects Ø6.2, without adding a cable to print exports or claiming flexible-wire mechanics.
